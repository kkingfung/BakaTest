# Game Proposal: Academic Battle Arena
## Inspired by "Baka to Test to Shoukanjuu" (笨蛋，測驗，召喚獸)

---

## 🎮 High Concept

A mobile competitive game that combines education with strategic auto-battler gameplay. Players earn attribute points by taking AI-generated academic tests, then strategically allocate those points to power up champions in PvP/PvE battles. Success requires both knowledge and tactical prowess.

**Core Loop:** Take Tests → Earn Points → Allocate to Champions → Battle → Earn Coins → Upgrade/Unlock → Repeat

---

## 📚 Core Mechanics

### 1. Test System

#### Structure
- **Format:** Multiple choice with 5 options per question
- **Questions per test:** Fixed amount (same for all grades)
- **Time limit:** Fixed duration (same for all grades, making higher grades harder)
- **Scoring:** Auto-submit when time expires, score only answered questions

#### Subjects & Difficulty
- **Core Subjects:** Math, Science, English, History
- **Difficulty Levels:** Grade-based (Elementary → Middle School → High School → University)
- **Higher grades = Higher points per question**
- **Player choice:** Select any subject/grade combination

#### Daily Limits
- **Unlimited test attempts** for practice
- **Limited score earning per subject** (total points cap per subject per day)
- **Same cap for all subjects** (encourages variety)
- **Promotes:** Learning through practice while preventing excessive grinding

#### AI Generation System
- **AI Generator:** Creates daily test pool for each subject/grade
- **AI Reviewer:** Validates questions, answers, and difficulty
- **Auto-publish:** If reviewer approves, tests go live automatically
- **Daily refresh:** New test pool generated each day, all players draw from same pool

---

### 2. Champion System

#### Collection Model (LoL-inspired)
- **Free champion rotation** (changes periodically)
- **Permanent unlock:** Direct purchase with in-battle coins or premium currency
- **No gacha for champions** (player-friendly)

#### Champion Abilities
- **Passive ability:** Always active
- **1 Active skill:** Main ability with strategic use
- **Simple design:** Optimized for mobile auto-battle

#### Champion Classification
- **Role system:** Tank, DPS, Support, Mage, Assassin, etc.
- **Elemental types:** Fire/Water/Earth/Wind (rock-paper-scissors advantages)
- **Additional systems:** Potential for more depth (e.g., faction bonuses)

#### Subject Affinity System (Unique Feature!)
- **Each champion has character-specific subject bonuses**
- Example: 
  - Champion A: Math gives 2x ATK, Science gives 1.5x DEF
  - Champion B: History gives 2x HP, English gives 2x SPD
- **Strategic depth:** Choose champions that match your strongest subjects

---

### 3. Point Allocation System

#### Strategic Resource Management
- **Before battle:** Players commit points from their earned pool
- **Point conversion:** Points → Champion stats based on character-specific ratios
- **All committed points are spent** (win or lose)
- **Self-balancing economy:** 
  - Going all-in wins battles but depletes resources
  - Must retake tests when points reach zero
  - Encourages strategic resource management

#### Preventing Pay-to-Win
- **No point purchases:** Can't buy test points with money
- **Skill matters:** RNG + strategy + point allocation creates depth
- **Comeback mechanics:** Zero-point players must retake tests (like the anime!)

---

### 4. Battle System

#### Combat Style
- **Auto-battle:** Champions fight automatically
- **Manual item use:** Players can tap to use consumable items during battle
- **Duration:** ~1 minute per battle round
- **Matches:** Can be single round or best-of-5 format

#### Battle Elements
- **Positioning/Formation:** Affects combat effectiveness
- **Champion counters:** Role and elemental advantages
- **Item builds:** Strategic consumable usage (healing, buffs, etc.)
- **Team synergies:** Champion combinations matter

#### RNG Elements (Excitement Factor)
- **Critical hits:** Chance for bonus damage
- **Dodge/Evasion:** Chance to avoid attacks
- **Ability procs:** Skills with % chance effects (e.g., 30% stun chance)
- **Balance:** Keeps combat exciting, prevents pure stat-check wins

#### Items & Consumables
- **Pre-battle loadout:** Equip consumables before match
- **Manual activation:** Tap to use during auto-battle
- **Types:** Healing potions, attack buffs, defensive shields, etc.
- **Visual gimmicks:** Cosmetic effects for flair

---

### 5. Game Modes

#### Battle Types (Participant-Based)
All modes use same battle mechanics, differ only in participant count:

1. **Individual Fight (1v1)**
   - Random matchmaking
   - Affects individual ranking
   - Quick matches

2. **Team Fight (5v5)**
   - Each player controls 1 champion
   - Random matchmaking or premade teams
   - Affects team coordination skills

3. **Guild Wars**
   - **Challenge-based** (like Baka to Test!)
   - Guilds can challenge specific rivals OR choose random for bonus rewards
   - Opt-in participation (not forced)
   - Rewards depend on event type
   - Multiple 5v5 battles simultaneously

4. **Kingdom Wars**
   - **Larger scale:** Multiple guilds fight for their kingdom
   - **Shorter duration:** Intense, explosive events
   - **Multiple simultaneous 5v5 battles**
   - Kingdom total score determines winner

5. **Simulation Fight**
   - **Zero stakes:** Set any points without spending them
   - **Casual/Fun:** Test strategies, try new champions
   - **Does not affect:** Individual ranking or resources

6. **PvE Practice Mode**
   - Fight AI opponents
   - Test strategies without risk
   - Learn champion mechanics

### 6. Progression Systems

#### Rankings (Multiple Leaderboards)
- **Individual PvP Ranking**
- **Individual Score Ranking** (total earned points across subjects)
- **Guild Ranking**
- **Kingdom Ranking**
- **Subject-Specific Rankings** (top Math player, Science master, etc.)

#### Ranking Impact (Baka to Test-inspired)
- **Visual world changes:** Higher-ranked guilds/kingdoms have better-looking facilities
- **Privilege unlocks:** Access to special low-point events
- **Social prestige:** Public displays (banners, monuments, guild halls)
- **Cosmetic upgrades:** Better environments, decorations, visual effects

#### Seasonal System (Monthly)
- **Duration:** 1 month per season
- **Soft reset:** Rankings reset or soft-reset
- **Persistence:** All owned champions, items, coins carry over
- **Fresh competition:** Everyone starts ranking fresh each month
- **Season rewards:** Exclusive cosmetics, titles, etc.

### 8. Monetization

#### Premium Currency Uses
1. **Champion Unlocks** (faster than grinding coins)
2. **Cosmetic Items** (skins, effects, emotes)
3. **Battle Pass** (seasonal progression rewards)
4. **Boosters** (increased daily score cap, faster coin earning)
5. **Gacha System** (for cosmetics/items, NOT champions)

#### Fair-to-Play Design
- **Cannot buy test points:** Skill and knowledge required
- **Champions available F2P:** Grindable with in-battle coins
- **Free rotation:** Try champions before buying
- **Cosmetics only:** Most premium items are visual
- **Boosters are convenience:** Not power (just faster progression)

#### In-Battle Coins (Earned Currency)
- **Earned from:** Winning battles
- **Used for:**
  - Champion purchases
  - Facility upgrades (guild/personal)
  - Consumable items
  - Gacha pulls
  - Various shop items

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: AI-Generated Test Quality
**Risk:** AI creates incorrect or inappropriate questions
**Mitigation:**
- Dual-AI review system
- Community reporting system
- Manual review of flagged questions
- Start with curated question bank, gradually introduce AI

### Risk 2: Subject Balance
**Risk:** One subject dominates meta (Math too strong, History too weak)
**Mitigation:**
- Regular balance patches
- Multiple champion designs per subject affinity
- Encourage diverse team compositions
- Monitor usage statistics

### Risk 3: Point Economy Exploitation
**Risk:** Players find ways to exploit point earning or cheating tests
**Mitigation:**
- Server-side validation
- Daily caps prevent excessive grinding
- Anti-cheat detection
- Irregular test pools (can't memorize answers)

### Risk 4: Low Player Base (Multiplayer Dependency)
**Risk:** Not enough players for matchmaking
**Mitigation:**
- AI opponents as fallback
- Bot players (clearly marked)
- Cross-region matchmaking
- Attractive PvE content

### Risk 5: Educational Value Questioned
**Risk:** Parents/educators don't see real learning benefit
**Mitigation:**
- Partner with education experts
- Align questions with curriculum standards
- Show player progress reports
- Case studies & testimonials

### Risk 6: Baka to Test IP Rights
**Risk:** Using IP without license could cause issues
**Mitigation:**
- **Option A:** Seek official license (costly but authentic)
- **Option B:** Create original IP with similar mechanics (safer)
- **Option C:** Launch as fan project, pivot if successful
